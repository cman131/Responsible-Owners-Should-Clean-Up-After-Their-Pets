Card Click — Interaction Pattern

What happens when you click a pokemon card in the bank?

A

Quick Action Popup

Clicking a card shows a small overlay with three buttons. Edit opens the full modal separately. Move to Team and Delete work in one click.

Full Editor Modal

Clicking any card opens the full pokemon editor in a modal. Move to Team and Delete are buttons in the modal header. One consistent interaction for everything.
